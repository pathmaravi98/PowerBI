📊 E-Commerce Dashboard – Power BI
🧾 Overview

This Power BI E-Commerce Dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of business performance, including sales, orders, profit, customer behavior, product performance, and payment insights.

The dashboard is fully interactive, allowing users to explore data through slicers and cross-filtering across multiple pages.

📄 Dashboard Pages & Visuals
🔹 1. Sales Overview

Slicers:

Month

Product Category

Payment Mode

City

Region

Key Metrics (Cards):

Total Sales

Total Orders

Total Quantity

Total Profit

Visuals:

Total Orders by Category (Clustered Column / Bar Chart)

Total Unit Price by Category (Bar Chart)

Monthly Order Trend (Line Chart)

Sales Distribution by Category (Pie / Donut Chart)

📌 Purpose:
Provides a quick snapshot of overall business performance, trends, and category distribution.

💳 2. Payment Insights

Visuals:

Revenue by Product Category (Treemap)

Revenue by Payment Mode (Bar Chart)

Payment Mode vs Profitability (Bar Chart)

Monthly Revenue Trend (Line Chart)

📌 Purpose:
Helps identify which payment methods drive revenue and profit, along with monthly performance trends.

📦 3. Customer, Product & Profitability

Visuals:

Revenue by Product Name (Bar / Treemap)

Profit Margin by Month (Line / Area Chart)

Profit Margin by Category (Bar Chart)

📌 Purpose:
Highlights top-performing products and analyzes profitability trends across time and categories.

🌍 4. Revenue by Location (Optional Map Page)

Visual:

Bubble Map

Configuration:

Location: City

Bubble Size: Total Revenue

Tooltip: Profit, Orders, Quantity

Legend (Optional): Category / Payment Mode

🎨 Design Settings:

Bubble Color: Brown Gradient (Light → Dark)

Shadow: Enabled

Background: Transparent

Page Color: White (#FFFFFF)

📌 Purpose:
Visualizes geographical sales distribution and regional performance.

🎨 Design & Formatting Guidelines

✅ Consistent theme across all pages

🎨 Page Background: White (#FFFFFF)

🌈 Color Scheme: Gradient / Theme-aligned

🟫 Shadows & Borders: Subtle and professional

🏷️ Data Labels: Minimal (use tooltips for clarity)

🔄 Interactivity: Enabled slicers & cross-filtering
